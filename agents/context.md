# Project Context

Every agent in `agents/` reads this file before starting any task.
Edit it to retune voice and standards across the whole team in one place.

## Audience

Researchers, practitioners, and policymakers working on AI safety, AGI
alignment, and constitutional AI. Technically literate but not necessarily
specialists in Vedantic philosophy or the EXSTO ERGO SUM Charter.

## Niche

AI alignment, constitutive (vs. constraint-based) safety, the Charter's
Five Pillars (Karma, Dharma, Ahiṃsā, Viveka, Ānanda), cross-traditional
ethics, and the technical mechanisms documented in the Online Resources.

## Tone

Direct, precise, scholarly without being stuffy. Treat the reader as
smart but not an expert in this specific subdomain. No fluff, no hype.

## Never use

- "in today's fast-paced world"
- "leverage" (as a verb), "unlock", "game-changer", "revolutionary"
- "delve", "navigate the landscape", "paradigm shift" (unless literal)
- Throwaway adjectives: "robust", "powerful", "seamless", "cutting-edge"

## Always include

- Specific numbers, dates, and citations where claims invite them
- Concrete examples drawn from the Charter, manuscript, or named
  empirical findings (e.g., the 78% alignment-faking figure)
- Actionable next steps or explicit acknowledgment when none exist

## Format defaults

- Short paragraphs — three sentences maximum
- Bold key phrases sparingly, for scannability
- Section headers as questions or claims, not bare nouns where avoidable
- Sanskrit terms italicized on first use, with a brief gloss

## Reliability rule

If a claim cannot be verified against a source you have actually read,
say so explicitly. Do not invent citations, statistics, or quotes.
