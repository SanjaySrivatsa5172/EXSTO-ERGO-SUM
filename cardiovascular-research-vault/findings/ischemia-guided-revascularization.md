# Ischemia-Guided Revascularization (the COURAGE/ISCHEMIA tension)

## In my words
The accumulated trial evidence that in *stable* coronary disease, an initial
invasive strategy (PCI/CABG + medical therapy) does not reduce death/MI versus
optimal medical therapy alone, even in patients with demonstrable moderate-to-severe
ischemia — though it does improve angina/quality of life. ISCHEMIA extended COURAGE
by enrolling on ischemia burden and still found no hard-outcome benefit from routine
early revascularization.

## Why it matters
It partially *contradicts* the intuition behind ischemia-targeting: if relieving
ischemia were the whole story, fixing more ischemia should cut events. That it
mostly improves symptoms reframes revascularization in stable CAD as a
symptom/quality-of-life decision, and pushes the event-prevention job toward
medical therapy acting on [[atherosclerosis]] and [[ldl-cholesterol-causality]].

## Strength & caveats
Large RCTs, but: stable patients only (not ACS), event rates lower than expected,
crossover and adherence issues, and left-main/very-high-risk subsets excluded.
"No benefit on average" is not "no benefit for anyone" — subgroup/mechanism
questions remain open.

## Connections
- [[fractional-flow-reserve]] — *refines/limits*: better lesion selection still didn't
  translate to fewer hard events in stable CAD.
- [[vulnerable-plaque]] — *explained-by*: events from non-flow-limiting lesions help
  explain why fixing ischemia doesn't prevent many MIs.
- [[ldl-cholesterol-causality]] — *complements*: shifts the event-prevention job toward
  medical therapy on wall biology.
- [[coronary-microvascular-dysfunction]] — *residual-cause*: part of the ischemia/symptoms
  left unaddressed by epicardial revascularization.

## Questions
- "No average benefit" ≠ "no benefit for anyone." Specify a subgroup where early
  revascularization plausibly does reduce events, and the trial that would isolate it.
- These trials enrolled stable patients. What changes in ACS, and what's the mechanistic
  reason the same strategy gives a different answer there?
