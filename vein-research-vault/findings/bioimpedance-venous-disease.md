# Bioimpedance & Venous Reflux Disease

## In my words
Bioimpedance (BIA / bioimpedance spectroscopy, BIS) passes a small current through
tissue; because extracellular fluid conducts differently from cells, the measured
impedance estimates **extracellular fluid volume** — an objective, quick, non-invasive
proxy for limb swelling. It is best **validated in [[lymphedema]]** (e.g. L-Dex). Its
use as an objective measure in *venous* oedema/reflux disease is more **exploratory**.

## Why it matters
Venous severity is starved of cheap *objective* swelling measures — most endpoints are
either subjective ([[vvsymq]]) or operator-intensive (water displacement, perometry,
APG/VFI). If bioimpedance tracked venous oedema reliably, it would give a fast objective
limb-fluid endpoint to sit alongside hemodynamic measures in
[[reflux-severity-evaluation]].

## Strength & caveats
Strong in lymphedema; **limited, smaller-scale venous-specific validation**. It
measures *total* extracellular fluid, so it can't by itself attribute swelling to venous
vs. lymphatic vs. lipedema causes ([[lipedema]] is non-fluid fat and may not register the
same way). Positioning, hydration, and bilateral disease (no normal contralateral
reference) all limit it. Treat venous claims cautiously and verify.

## Connections
- [[lymphedema]] — *validated-in*: the condition where BIA evidence is strongest.
- [[reflux-severity-evaluation]] — *candidate-objective-axis*: a possible fast swelling
  endpoint, not yet established for venous.
- [[lipedema]] — *limits*: fat-based swelling confounds a fluid-based measure.
- [[qol-parameters-venous-disease]] — *objective-counterpart*: pairs an objective fluid
  measure against subjective PROs.

## Questions
- Before trusting BIA as a venous oedema endpoint, what validation study (against which
  reference, in which population) would you require, and what would falsify it?
- BIA reads total extracellular fluid. In a phlebolymphedema limb, how would you separate
  the venous-attributable component of a change from the lymphatic one?
